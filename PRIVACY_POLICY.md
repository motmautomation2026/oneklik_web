## 1. Who This Applies To

This policy covers two different kinds of data, and they are handled differently:

1. **Your account data** — information about you, as a registered user of One-Klik.
2. **Business contact data** — company and people information you search for and reveal through the Service, which relates to third parties (not you).

## 2. Information We Collect

### Account information
- **Sign-up details**: your email address and password (we never see or store your password in plain text — authentication is handled by our infrastructure provider, Supabase, using industry-standard hashing).
- **Google sign-in**: if you use "Continue with Google," we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google.
- **Onboarding details**: your company name, role, and intended use case.
- **Billing details**: legal name, entity type (business/individual), GSTIN (if applicable), and address — collected once you make a purchase, used to generate tax invoices.

### Payment information
- Payments are processed by **Razorpay**. We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves.
- We retain records of the transaction itself: amount, credits purchased, plan, payment status, and invoice/receipt documents.

### Usage information
- Your searches, saved lists, credit balance, and transaction history.
- Support requests you raise with us, including any file attachments.
- Standard technical logs (e.g. IP address, browser/device information, timestamps) generated by normal use of a web application.

### Business contact data (third-party data, not yours)
When you use Company Search, People Search, LinkedIn Lookup, or reveal an email/phone number, One-Klik returns business contact information about companies and professionals — sourced from data partners and enrichment providers, not collected directly from those individuals by us. This data is provided to help you conduct legitimate business outreach. **You are responsible for using it in compliance with applicable law** (e.g. anti-spam and data protection laws in your own jurisdiction) when contacting the people or companies returned by a search.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the Service (searches, reveals, lists, credits).
- Process payments and issue tax invoices/receipts.
- Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- Respond to support requests.
- Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or violations of our terms.
- Improve the Service and understand how it's used.
- Send you service-related communications (e.g. email confirmation, password reset, payment confirmations, support replies).

We do **not** sell your account data to third parties.

## 4. Who We Share Information With

We share information only as needed to operate the Service:

| Who | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Account data, usage data | Our database, authentication, and file storage infrastructure |
| **Razorpay** | Payment amount, your billing/contact details needed for checkout | Payment processing |
| **Data enrichment partners** | Your search query terms (not personal account data) | Sourcing company/people search results |
| **Google** | Nothing beyond standard OAuth sign-in flow | "Continue with Google" sign-in, if you choose to use it |

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of One-Klik, our users, or others.

## 5. Data Retention

- Account data is retained for as long as your account is active.
- Financial records (invoices, payment records) are retained as required by applicable tax and accounting law — typically several years — even after account closure.
- Credit ledger entries are retained permanently as an audit trail of your account's transaction history.
- If you close your account, contact us at **[SUPPORT/PRIVACY EMAIL]** to request deletion of data we are not otherwise required to retain.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:
- Database-level access controls (row-level security) so your data is isolated from other accounts.
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic to and from the Service.
- Payment processing handled entirely by a PCI-DSS-compliant payment gateway (Razorpay) — we never touch raw card data.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to the Service.
