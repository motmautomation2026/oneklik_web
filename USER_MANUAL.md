## 1. Getting Started

### Creating an account

1. Go to the sign-up page and enter your work email and a password (at least 8 characters).
2. You can also sign up with **Continue with Google** instead.
3. After signing up, check your inbox for a confirmation email. It contains two ways to confirm:
   - Click the **Confirm email address** button, or
   - Copy the **6-digit code** and enter it on the "Check your inbox" screen in the app, along with your email.
4. Once confirmed, you'll land on a short one-step onboarding form: your **company name**, **role**, and **what you'll use One-Klik for**. This takes a few seconds and unlocks the rest of the app.
5. New accounts start with a small grant of free credits so you can try a search or reveal right away.

## 2. Logging In

- Enter your email and password, or use **Continue with Google**.
- **Forgot your password?** Click "Forgot password?" on the login screen, enter your email, and you'll receive a 6-digit reset code. Enter that code along with a new password on the next screen. You can resend the code if it doesn't arrive (a short cooldown applies between resends).

## 3. Dashboard

After logging in, you land on the Dashboard — a quick-access home screen with cards for:

- **Company Search**
- **People Search**
- **LinkedIn Lookup**
- **Lists**

...plus a shortcut to buy more credits if you're running low.

## 4. Company Search

Use this to find companies matching specific criteria.

**Manual filters:**
- **Industry** — type and pick from live suggestions, or add your own.
- **Location** — same, with live suggestions.
- **Company size** — pick one or more size ranges (e.g. 11-20, 201-500, 10000+).
- **Companies to fetch** — how many results you want (1–100). The estimated credit cost updates as you change this.
- Click **Find Companies**.

**AI Mode:** click the **AI Mode** toggle to switch to a single free-text box — describe what you're looking for in a sentence (e.g. *"Automotive companies in Pune with 500+ employees"*) and click **Search**. AI Mode searches are capped at 25 results, cost only 1 credit if anything is found, and are free if nothing matches.

**Results:** each row shows Company, Website (clickable), Location, Industry, Headcount, Phone, and a LinkedIn link. Once you have results, click **Save to list** to store them for later — you'll be asked to name the new list.

## 5. People Search

Find people at specific companies.

**Manual filters:**
- **Company domains** — e.g. `zf.com`.
- **Job titles** — with live suggestions.
- **Location** — with live suggestions.
- **Count per company** — how many people to fetch per company domain.
- Click **Find People**.

**AI Mode:** same idea as Company Search — describe who you're looking for in one sentence.

**Revealing contact info:** each person's Email and Phone columns start hidden behind a **Reveal** button.
- Click **Reveal** on an individual row, or select multiple rows with the checkboxes and use the bulk **Reveal Email** / **Reveal Phone** buttons above the table.
- **You are only charged if a result is actually found.** An empty result costs nothing.
- If you select more rows than your credit balance can cover, One-Klik reveals as many as it can afford and tells you how many were skipped — it never fails the whole batch over a partial shortfall.

**Save to list:** same as Company Search — click **Save to list** once you have results.

## 6. LinkedIn Lookup

Paste one or more LinkedIn profile URLs to find the associated email and phone number.

1. Paste a URL into the input and press Enter (repeat for multiple URLs).
2. Click **Search**.
3. The lookup itself is always free. Every profile automatically checks both email and phone:
   - Email found: charged
   - Phone found: charged
   - Both found: charged for both
   - Nothing found: completely free
4. If your credit balance can't cover every result found, you'll see a note showing how many were skipped for that reason.
5. Results can be saved to a list just like the other search pages.

## 7. Lists

Every "Save to list" action from a search, and every LinkedIn Lookup, creates or adds to a list. From the **Lists** page you can:

- **Open** a list to see its full rows.
- **Rename** or **Delete** a list.
- **Export to CSV** from a list's detail page.
- **Merge lists**: select two or more lists of the *same kind* (company or people) on the Lists page and click **Merge** — this creates a brand-new list containing every row from the selected lists with duplicates removed. The original lists are left untouched.
- **Re-run enrichment on a saved list** (people lists only): select rows inside a list and use **Reveal Email** / **Reveal Phone** — rows that already have that field are skipped automatically, so you're only ever charged for rows that still need it.

## 8. Wallet & Credits

Your **Wallet** page shows:

- **Available** credits — what you can spend right now.
- **Held** credits — temporarily reserved while a search or reveal is in progress.
- **Lifetime purchased** and **lifetime consumed** totals.
- A full, filterable **transaction history** (credit vs. debit) showing exactly what every credit was spent on or where it came from.

A **Low balance** badge appears in the top bar once your available balance drops below a small threshold, so you're never surprised mid-search.

## 9. Monthly Plans & Subscriptions

One-Klik runs on **monthly subscription plans** (Starter, Growth, Business), each granting a set number of credits per month. Open **Plans** (linked from the Dashboard, Wallet, and sidebar) to subscribe or manage your plan.

**Subscribing for the first time:**
1. **Billing details required first.** If you haven't added billing details yet (legal name, address, state, postal code — GSTIN optional), you'll be prompted to fill them in before checkout. This only needs to be done once.
2. Pick a plan and click **Subscribe**. This opens Razorpay's secure checkout — pay by card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet.
3. Once payment is confirmed, your monthly credits are added automatically and an invoice is generated.
4. Every plan card lists exactly what's included (search, reveal, LinkedIn Lookup, AI search, lists) plus billing terms (billed monthly, credits after payment confirmation, rollover rules, a 2-day buffer after the due date).
5. Need a custom volume? The **Enterprise** card lets you contact sales directly instead of subscribing to a fixed plan.

**Renewing, upgrading, or downgrading:**
- Near your renewal date, a **pro forma invoice** (an unpaid billing document) is issued for your upcoming payment.
- The Plans page shows a banner with your current plan, its status, when the current period ends, and the buffer deadline. Click **Pay now — renew** to pay it.
- Picking the **same plan or a higher plan** when you pay carries over any unused credits. **Downgrading to a cheaper plan does not** roll over leftover credits.
- If payment isn't made by the due date, you get a short **2-day buffer** before the plan is treated as past due — plenty of time to sort out a failed card or similar.
- Buttons on each plan card change to reflect your situation: **Subscribe** (no active plan), **Renew plan** (same plan), or **Switch & pay** (a different plan).

## 10. Billing Hub

Everything related to billing lives under **Billing** (from your account menu), across three tabs:

- **Invoices** — every invoice and pro forma document you've received, with spend totals at the top. Filter by **All / Paid / Payment due / Past due**. Click **Details** on any row to view or download that document; unpaid ones show a direct **Pay now** button.
- **Billing details** — your legal name, GSTIN (optional), and address used on future invoices. Editing this never changes documents already issued. Payments themselves are always collected via Razorpay at checkout — no card is stored on file.
- **Plan** — your current plan, monthly credit amount, period-end date, buffer deadline, and a link to any outstanding pro forma invoice, plus a button to renew or change plans.

## 11. Profile & Password

From your account menu → **Profile**, you can see your account details (company, role, use case) and:

- **Change your password** at any time from the same page.
- **Sign out** of your account.

## 12. Support

Need help? Use the support icon in the top bar, or go to **Support**:

- **Raise a request**: pick a category, write a subject and description, optionally attach a screenshot or PDF (up to a few files, 5 MB each), and submit.
- You'll get a reply in the same conversation thread — no separate email chain. A red badge on the support icon shows when there's a new reply waiting.
- Replying to a resolved request within a short window reopens it automatically; older resolved requests should be raised as a new request instead (it'll stay linked to the original for context).

## 13. Account Status Notices

If your account is ever restricted, you'll see a clear notice explaining why and what to do:

- **Frozen**: you can still view your existing data, but spending credits (searches, reveals, purchases) is paused. You can still raise a support request to ask about it.
- **Suspended / Banned**: you'll see a dedicated screen explaining the restriction, with a direct way to raise a support request to appeal — this is always available, even when the rest of the app is locked, so you're never stuck without a way to reach us.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

**Do my unused credits carry over when I renew?**
Yes, if you renew the same plan or upgrade to a higher one. Downgrading to a cheaper plan does not carry over leftover credits.

**Why was I charged for a search that returned nothing?**
You shouldn't be. Company/People searches only bill if something is delivered; reveals (email, phone, LinkedIn) only bill per row actually found. If you believe you were charged incorrectly, check your Wallet's transaction history and raise a support request with the details.

**What happens if I don't have enough credits for a whole batch reveal?**
One-Klik reveals as many rows as your balance can cover and clearly tells you how many were skipped — it never fails the entire batch over a partial shortfall.

**Can I get an invoice for my purchase?**
Yes — every payment generates a downloadable invoice, available from your account menu under Billing → Invoices.

**What's a pro forma invoice?**
It's an unpaid billing document issued ahead of a renewal, showing what you owe before you pay. Once paid, it's replaced by a regular paid invoice.

**I think my account was restricted by mistake — what do I do?**
Raise a support request. This is always reachable, even if your account is frozen, suspended, or banned.
